Handwriting / handwriting stroke data

The Chinese strokes in src/chalk/glyphs.json use the medians from Hanzi Writer
Data v2.0.1 (https://github.com/chanind/hanzi-writer-data), sourced from Make Me
a Hanzi (https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi). The original fonts are
copyright (C) 1999 Arphic Technology Co., Ltd. These character data and the
bundled subset are distributed under the Arphic Public License. See the full,
unmodified license in ARPHICPL.TXT. The generated file carries its modification
date and description; its editable source data are included in the template.

The compiler tools/build_chalk_glyphs.py selects characters and preserves their
ordered medians. The renderer flips the source coordinates vertically to lay
them out on an SVG board. Latin and mathematical pen strokes, the board,
the composition, and the chalk sounds are authored independently.
